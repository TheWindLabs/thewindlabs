Who we are

Training the innovators who will shape aviation's future

Founded by two aviation enthusiasts in 2022, our vision is to pioneer the future of aviation and drone technology — training the next generation of innovators, expanding the applications of drones across industries, and equipping future leaders with the skills to shape a smarter, safer and more connected world.

  • “Catch them young.” Inspiring young learners with structured, hands-on courses in aeromodelling and drone technology.

Why aeromodelling & drones

More than a hobby — a workout for hands and mind

Building and flying aircraft is known to improve hand–eye coordination, fine motor skills, spatial awareness and precise control — crucial for both manual dexterity and cognitive processing.

Flight control

Flying RC planes or drones demands real-time adjustments, sharpening reaction speed and coordination.

Problem-solving under pressure

Quick mid-flight corrections build decision-making, creativity and spatial awareness.

Simulator training

Practice on flight simulators builds muscle memory and reflexes that carry into real-world piloting.

Practical engineering

Mechanics, electronics and material science in one project — ideal for aspiring STEM enthusiasts.

Level 1 · Starter

Chuck Gliders

An entry-level introductory course for budding aviation enthusiasts.

What it covers

  • Introduction to aeromodelling
  • Theory of flight
  • Chuck glider construction and flying
  • Starter course for aeromodelling and flying
Level 2 · Motorised

Multirotors

Introduces the concept, operations and applications of a quadcopter — and the participants' first motorised aircraft.

What you'll learn

  • Design, structure and flight dynamics, and how they differ from fixed-wing aircraft
  • Electronics, sensors and mission planner software
  • Hovering and stabilisation in practice
  • Regulatory and compliance guidelines
  • Applications in defence and career opportunities
Outcome

On certification, participants can identify, design, select components for and assemble multirotors in-house to suit project or mission parameters. This unit also introduces AI modules in drones.

Level 3 · Fixed-wing

RC Planes

Electric-powered fixed-wing flight — the stepping stone to advanced drone technologies.

What you'll learn

  • Electric propulsion systems and basic avionics
  • Control surfaces
  • Communication basics, including pairing and calibration
  • Battery management
  • Safety rules and regulations (as per DGCA)
  • Basic flight training
  • Applications in defence and career opportunities
Outcome

On certification, participants can assemble a basic RC fixed-wing aircraft, maintain it and test-fly it with instructors. Flying solo requires further training and practice — and opens opportunities in defence, industrial and commercial work.
